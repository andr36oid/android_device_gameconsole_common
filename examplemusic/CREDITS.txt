Example music
=============

A few lo-fi tracks so the music player has something to play. They are yours to
keep or delete; andr36oid copies them here only once, on the first start.

All of them are from the Free Music Archive (freemusicarchive.org). The CC0
tracks are in the public domain. The CC BY 4.0 tracks may be shared and changed
as long as the artist is credited. andr36oid changed them all the same way: they
were re-encoded to 128 kbit/s MP3 to save space, and the tags were tidied.
