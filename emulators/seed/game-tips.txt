GAME TIPS FOR THE HEAVY SYSTEMS (andr36oid)

This console has a slow processor. Most games for the older systems run at
full speed. For PlayStation, N64, PSP and Dreamcast, some games need a
setting, and some are too heavy. This list says which. It is based on what
people found on other consoles with the same chip (RK3326: R36S, RG351,
ArkOS, ROCKNIX). Your results may differ a little.

Already set for you on a new console:
- Donkey Kong 64 (N64): a fix for the camera. It works when the ROM file has
  its usual name, for example "Donkey Kong 64 (USA).z64".
- God of War: Chains of Olympus and Ghost of Sparta (PSP): run at 30
  frames per second instead of 60. The game stays at normal speed, but
  it looks less smooth.
- Many other games get fixes inside the emulators themselves. You don't
  need to do anything for those.


PLAYSTATION (RetroArch, PCSX ReARMed)
- Almost every game runs at full speed. A few heavy ones, such as Gran
  Turismo and Ape Escape, can slow down.
- If a game stutters, the emulator skips a frame now and then by itself.
- European games with copy protection (for example Vagrant Story, Spyro 3,
  Final Fantasy VIII PAL) need their .sbi file next to the game, with the
  same name.
- Games on more than one disc: make an .m3u file that lists the discs, and
  start that file.


NINTENDO 64 (RetroArch, Mupen64Plus-Next)
- Lighter games such as Super Mario 64 and Mario Kart 64 should run well.
- Big games like Donkey Kong 64 and Majora's Mask slow down in busy scenes.
- Too heavy: Perfect Dark, Conker's Bad Fur Day, Jet Force Gemini,
  Ridge Racer 64, GoldenEye with 3 or 4 players.
- The picture is 320x240 to keep the speed up. Some glow effects (the sun
  and torches in Zelda) are missing on purpose. To get them back for one
  game: Quick Menu > Core Options > Depth buffer to RDRAM > Software, then
  Core Options > Manage Core Options > Save Game Options. It costs speed.


PSP (PPSSPP)
- Full speed or close: Crisis Core, Kingdom Hearts Birth by Sleep,
  Persona 3 Portable, Monster Hunter Freedom Unite, Final Fantasy Tactics,
  GTA Chinatown Wars, Need for Speed Most Wanted.
- Too heavy or slow: Tekken 6, Burnout Legends and Dominator, WipEout Pure
  and Pulse, Silent Hill Origins, God of War (playable at 30 fps).
- GTA Liberty City Stories and Vice City Stories: slow in busy streets.
  Don't turn on "Skip buffer effects" for GTA, God of War, Metal Gear
  Peace Walker or Silent Hill: the picture breaks.
- To try a setting for one game only: in the game, open the PPSSPP menu,
  pick "Create game config", then change the setting. Note: PPSSPP then
  keeps the buttons for that game too.


DREAMCAST (Flycast, as tested on other consoles with this chip)
- Run well: Crazy Taxi, Sonic Adventure 1 and 2, Soulcalibur, Jet Set
  Radio, Marvel vs. Capcom 2, Ikaruga, Power Stone, Virtua Fighter 3tb.
- Playable: Dead or Alive 2, Rez, Grandia II, Skies of Arcadia,
  Virtua Tennis, Power Stone 2.
- Too heavy: Sega Rally 2, Resident Evil 2, F355 Challenge.


SUPER NINTENDO (RetroArch, Snes9x 2010)
- Games with extra chips (Star Fox, Yoshi's Island, Doom, Super Mario RPG,
  Kirby Super Star) are the heaviest. Star Fox was slow on the real
  console too. If the sound crackles, the emulator skips a frame.


NOT SUPPORTED
- Nintendo DS: the fast DS emulator (DraStic) is closed and can't come with
  the console. Others are too slow on this chip.
- Sega Saturn: not included. A few 2D games would run, most 3D games don't.
- PlayStation 2, GameCube, Wii: far too heavy.
